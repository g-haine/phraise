## Abstract
We consider port-Hamiltonian systems from a geometric perspective, where the quantities involved such as state, flows, and efforts evolve in (possibly infinite-dimensional) Banach spaces. The main contribution of this article is the introduction of a weak solution concept. In this framework we show that a suitable part of the state is weakly differentiable in a space that, for ordinary evolution equations, plays the role of an extrapolation space. Through examples, we demonstrate that this approach is consistent with the weak solution framework commonly used for partial differential equations.
