## Abstract
Precise modeling of magnetorheological clutches for collaborative robotics is challenged by coupled hysteresis induced by current and speed excitation. To address this dual-input hysteresis behavior, this article proposes a mechanism-guided multiphysics parametric state-space model formulated within the port-Hamiltonian (PH) framework. Based on this mechanism-guided formulation, low-order internal-state submodels are constructed for ferromagnetic magnetization and the viscoelastic–shear dynamics of the magnetorheological fluid, where the magnetization–viscoelasticity module characterizes current-induced hysteresis and the enhanced LuGre-type shear module captures speed-induced hysteresis near reversals. To explicitly represent interdomain energy exchange, these submodels are integrated into a coupled parametric state-space form using a PH representation with explicit storage–dissipation separation. Passivity constraints are derived from the PH power-balance property and incorporated into the identification process. Experiments under both periodic and aperiodic dual-input excitations demonstrate substantially improved prediction accuracy compared with a modified Bouc–Wen–Bingham hybrid benchmark. Further tests under bounded input and parameter perturbations indicate reduced performance degradation. Internal-state evolution further supports the attribution of current- and speed-induced memory effects.
